Why Class Manager

Everything a student needs — organised for them, not against them.

Before, students juggled WhatsApp links, random PDFs and missed classes. Class Manager replaces all of it with one clear, guided experience that keeps every learner on track for their NIOS exam.

Never miss a class

A personal, chapter-wise timetable with live-class links and a countdown means students always know what to study and when.

See real progress

Every lecture watched, DPP done and test attempted builds a live progress score — so students stay motivated and parents can see effort.

Get unstuck fast

Any doubt goes straight to the right subject teacher with a photo or voice note — answers come back inside the app, not lost in a group chat.

Getting started

From joining to studying — in three simple steps.

This is exactly what a new student experiences from the moment they join MVS Foundation.

Welcome on WhatsApp

As soon as a student joins, we send a warm welcome message on WhatsApp with their login details and the app link. Their phone number is their login — nothing to remember.

Starts on WhatsApp

Log in with your phone

Open the app, enter your 10-digit phone number, and set your password. Forgot it later? A one-time code (OTP) is sent to WhatsApp to reset it instantly.

One-tap secure login

Complete your profile

A short, guided setup — photo, class, exam session, batch, medium and subjects. This one step personalises the entire app for the student.
